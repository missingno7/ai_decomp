# Applying the memory to the active projects

These are **GENERIC METHOD** recommendations and **WORKING HYPOTHESES** to test
in the owning projects. This extraction has not edited or promoted anything
there. Sources are the named mechanisms/trajectories in the
[catalog](../knowledge/mechanisms.md) and four linked dossiers.

**Stunts:** keep the camera BP-home finding separate from the unresolved extent
and return-lowering residue. The known return-label fixture and new
negative-polarity result remove specific low-information retries; they do not
rule out every CFG or TU context. Export parent/oracle deltas and effective
classes with complete compiler/card identity. Measure supervisor overhead and
novel outcomes before paying for a larger routing cohort. SimAnt's collapse
discipline and Icy's controlled predecessor probes are useful experimental
precedents, without importing their compiler mechanisms.

**SimAnt:** retain the current-profile blocker reclassification and use the
DrawForSale trajectory as a compact worker example of why body convergence can
isolate a private binding question. For the small exact-body subgroup, verified
unit/selector/private-data assembly is a better-evidenced next step than another
expression sweep. For the much larger source-shape subgroup, require a new
predicted compiler effect instead of assuming a declaration swap will generalize.
Empires' body-versus-topology distinction is a useful proof analogy, not a
replacement for Win16 semantic fixups.

**Icy Tower:** preserve historical DWARF scope/order evidence and exact neighbors
while separating target liveness/CFG questions from source spellings that
converge. The `play` pass trace identifies where a distinction disappears;
the `draw_frame` residue needs reaching-value/path evidence. Predecessor
dependency is measured, but the precise persistent compiler-state explanation
still needs isolation. Empires' whole-TU route probe illustrates controlled
context variation, not the same GCC cause.

The highest-leverage shared next task is a small read-only metadata adapter
cohort across these three projects: prove that the shared identity contract
reproduces native grouping without losing fixups/context, then measure whether
the resulting handoff actually reduces redundant trials and supervisor work.
Keep compiler services, object parsers and acceptance gates local until that
evidence suggests a narrower common interface.
